MODULE 3.3 : EVALUATING CONTENT

Information vs. Misinformation

How to identify what you can trust

✅

Reliable Facts

Verified Content
  • Has clear, cited sources and author details.
  • Reported by multiple reputed news agencies.
  • Neutral tone without emotional manipulation.
  • Rule: "Cross-reference before believing."
⚠️

Misinformation

Fake News & Deepfakes
  • Clickbait titles designed to trigger anger or fear.
  • AI-generated images (Deepfakes) or manipulated videos.
  • Shared primarily via untraceable social media chains.
  • Rule: "If it looks too shocking to be true, verify it."
